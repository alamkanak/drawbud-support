A little help. A fresh start.

We’re here to help you get back to drawing.

Contact support

Start with paper

Drawbud guides you through drawings on your own paper. Explore a drawing or follow a Journey. Each lesson downloads its drawing, teaching visuals, and voice guidance together.

Learn at your own pace

Use Previous, Next, and Replay. Turn on hands-free learning to watch each demonstration, draw during a practice pause, and continue automatically. Change practice time, captions, and voice guidance in lesson settings.

Downloads and offline practice

Find downloaded lessons in Library → Offline. Downloads need a connection; verified downloaded lessons work offline. If a download fails, check your connection and use Try again. Removing a download does not erase your cloud practice history.

Accounts and progress

Start as a guest. An optional account keeps practice across devices. Confirm your email when asked. Signing in transfers your guest progress. You can reset a forgotten password from Settings.

Delete an account

Open Drawbud → Settings → Delete account and confirm. The account, saved drawing references, and cloud practice history are removed. If you cannot access the app, email alam.kanak@gmail.com. Never post passwords, tokens, or account details in public issues.

Tell us what happened

Email your device model, operating system, Drawbud version, and the steps that led to the problem. A screenshot can help; leave out private information. We never need your password.
