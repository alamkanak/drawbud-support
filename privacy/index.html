Privacy, plainly.

Effective October 27, 2026. This policy describes the current version of Drawbud, a general-audience drawing education app. There are currently no subscriptions, advertising, or third-party analytics.

Contact support

Information Drawbud uses

Guest use creates a private random user identifier. The service stores lesson progress, practice attempts, journey enrollment, and saved drawing references under that identifier. If you choose to create an account, Supabase Auth also processes your email and password. Passwords are not included in app diagnostics or provided to support staff.

What stays on your device

Downloaded lessons, display and learning preferences, and pending offline progress stay on your device. Drawbud does not photograph, record, or upload drawings you make on paper. We do not request camera, microphone, location, or contacts access.

Why information is used

Identifiers and lesson activity let the app remember your place, maintain saved drawings, calculate journey participation, and sync between your devices. Notifications are optional local reminders. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Service providers

Supabase provides authentication, database, storage, and the API. Network requests necessarily expose technical information such as IP addresses to the provider. See Supabase’s privacy policy. GitHub Pages hosts this site and may process routine website access logs; see GitHub’s privacy statement. This site uses no analytics scripts, tracking pixels, or advertising cookies. Creator Studio uses browser storage for its administrator session and account-specific draft recovery copies.

Retention and deletion

Account information and practice history remain while your account is active. Deleting your account in Settings removes the account and its owned application records. Provider backups and operational logs follow provider retention schedules. Downloads remain on your device until removed or the app is deleted. Guest data belongs to a private device session; linking to an account makes practice recoverable across devices.

Your choices

You may use guest access, disable audio or captions, remove downloads, decline notifications, or delete your account. For access, correction, deletion, or other privacy questions, email alam.kanak@gmail.com. We may need to verify that a request concerns your own account. Never send a password or access token.

Families

Drawbud is a general-audience app for adults and families, rather than an Apple Kids Category app. A parent or guardian should manage accounts used by younger children. If personal information was provided inappropriately, contact us so we can investigate and remove it.

Changes

We will update this policy if data practices change. A future subscription feature will require a separate release and updated purchase and privacy information.
