Before your first mark

A few things you might be wondering.

Do I draw on the screen?

Drawbud guides you while you draw on your own paper. Your phone or iPad shows the demonstration and keeps your place in the lesson.

Do I need special materials?

A pencil and paper are a good place to start. Each lesson lists the materials you’ll need, so you can get ready before you begin.

Is this for beginners?

Yes. We’re building the first lessons around careful observation, simple shapes, and a light touch. The aim is a growing path from foundations to more developed drawing practice.

When can I try Drawbud?

Drawbud is in development, and the first lessons are taking shape. We’ll share availability here when it’s ready. In the meantime, you can reach us through Support.
